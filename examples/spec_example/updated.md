# **Nimbus Analytics Product Guide**

Welcome to Nimbus. We are pleased to have you here. This guide provides comprehensive instructions covering everything from setting up your initial dashboard to utilizing the API. It is extensive, so please feel free to take your time and navigate to the sections most relevant to you.

Nimbus is a cloud analytics platform that enables you to connect your data, analyze it, and share insights with your team. Since its inception in 2019, over 4,000 companies have adopted it for daily use. Our mission is to make analytics straightforward and effective; should any part of this guide be unclear, we take full responsibility.

# **Getting Started**

Getting started is straightforward. Register at https://nimbus.example/signup, verify your email address, and you will gain access. A credit card is not required for the 14-day trial period, and you may invite up to 5 colleagues immediately.

- 1 Create a workspace and assign it a name recognizable to your team
- 2 Connect your initial data source (Postgres, Snowflake, BigQuery, among others)
- 3 Construct a dashboard using one of our 30 available templates
- 4 Share the dashboard with your team and celebrate your accomplishment

Upon completing these steps, you will have acquired the fundamental skills necessary to use the platform. The remainder of this guide provides a more detailed exploration of each component.

# **Pricing**

We have endeavored to maintain straightforward pricing, devoid of hidden fees or unexpected charges. Each plan is billed <u>monthly per workspace, and you may change plans at your discretion.</u>

|Plan|Price per month|Included seats|Data rows|
|---|---|---|---|
|Starter|$31.90|3|1 million|
|Pro|$86.90|10|50 million|
|Enterprise|$273.90|Unlimited|Unlimited|

The Starter plan is ideal for those who are evaluating the service, the Pro plan is preferred by most expanding teams, and the Enterprise plan is designed for organizations requiring SSO, audit logs, and a dedicated success manager. Additional seats on Starter and Pro plans are priced at $8.80 each per month. Annual payments include a benefit of two months free, which represents a considerable saving.

Nonprofit organizations and educational institutions are eligible for a 50% discount on any plan. Please contact us via email at billing@nimbus.example to arrange this.

# **API**

Nimbus offers a comprehensive REST API that enables automation of virtually all actions available within the application. An API key is required, which can be obtained from the Settings under the Developer section.

## **API v2**

The v2 API is the current version and is recommended for all new development. It utilizes JSON over HTTPS, supports pagination via cursors, and enforces a rate limit of 600 requests per minute per workspace. The complete reference documentation is available at https://docs.nimbus.example/api/v2.

```
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $NIMBUS_KEY" https://api.nimbus.example/v2/dashboards
```

## **Webhooks**

Webhooks enable Nimbus to notify your systems when specific events occur, such as an alert being triggered or a report completing. You may configure up to 20 webhook endpoints per workspace, and we will retry failed deliveries up to 5 times using exponential backoff.

# **Security and Compliance**

We prioritize security with the utmost seriousness, as it is central to our mission. All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit with TLS version 1.2 or higher. Nimbus holds SOC 2 Type II certification and undergoes annual third-party penetration testing.

Enterprise customers benefit from Single Sign-On (SSO) via SAML or OIDC, SCIM provisioning, and audit logs retained for 365 days. Should you require a custom data processing agreement, please contact us, and our legal team will facilitate the arrangement.
